01 · What verification confirms

Three questions, answered against the laboratory's own record.

Our certificates state that they are valid only as currently shown on this site and may be withdrawn. Verification is how you see that current state.

01

Was it issued by us?

The report number and private verify code must match a certificate in our records. Unknown numbers or wrong codes fail — the same way, so report numbers can't be probed.

02

Is it unaltered?

We keep the cryptographic fingerprint of every PDF we release. Drop your copy and we compare fingerprints: change a single character and it no longer matches.

03

Is it still current?

If a certificate is reissued or withdrawn, verification says so, gives the reason, and points to the replacement report.

04 · Reading a report

What each figure means.

Every value on a certificate traces back to raw instrument data. These are the terms you'll see on the verified report.

Identity
The principal component, confirmed against a reference standard by its MRM transitions (quantifier and qualifier) and retention time on the triple-quadrupole.
Net peptide content
The mass of peptide actually present in the vial as received, in mg — quantified against an external calibration curve.
Peptide fill accuracy
Measured net content ÷ declared label claim × 100. Shown as “—” when no label claim was declared.
Chromatographic purity
Principal-component peak area (Ph. Eur. 2.2.46). It bounds peptide-related impurities only — not excipients, counter-ions or water — and is separate from net content.
Result vs limit
Heavy-metal and endotoxin results are shown against their stated limits (ICH Q3D parenteral PDE; specified EU/mL limit) with a conformance status.
No pass/fail on peptide reports
Identity, purity and content values are reported as measured. Peptide certificates make no pass/fail determination against any specification or label claim.
Common questions

Verification, answered.

A report failed verification. What does that mean?

Either the details were mistyped, or the certificate was not issued by SideChain Analytics. Check the report number (including any -HM or -EN suffix) and the verify code. If it still fails, please tell us at info@sidechainanalytics.com — we track forged certificates.

The report is genuine, but my PDF doesn't match. Why?

The PDF has been changed since we released it — even re-saving or editing a single figure changes its fingerprint. Rely on the results shown on the verified report page, or download the original directly from us there.

What does “superseded” or “withdrawn” mean?

Superseded: the certificate was reissued, usually to correct a detail; the page links to the current report. Withdrawn: the certificate must no longer be relied on. Either way, the reason is shown.

Does my PDF get uploaded?

No. Your browser computes the file's SHA-256 fingerprint locally and sends only that 64-character value. The document itself never leaves your device.

Does a verified report certify a product or lot?

No. Results relate only to the sample as received and the tests performed — not to any other unit, vial, lot or batch, or any product bearing the same name. Check that the lot on your vial matches the report.

Can I share the verification link?

Yes — the link contains the report number and verify code, so anyone with it can view the report. Share it only with people you'd share the certificate with.
