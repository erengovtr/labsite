Understanding our report

From transition to result.

Every figure on your Certificate of Analysis traces back to raw instrument data. Here is how HPLC-MS/MS turns a single vial into the numbers you read.

01

Separate

Liquid chromatography holds back each compound to a different degree, so the peptides leave the column one at a time — each at its own retention time.

02

Identify

Tandem MS selects the intact peptide by its mass, fragments it, and re-selects one specific fragment — a transition. Two are followed: a quantifier (measures amount) and a qualifier (confirms identity).

03

Quantify

The quantifier's chromatographic peak is integrated; its area — read against calibrated standards and scaled through the sample prep — becomes the measured milligrams, and the recovery.

Common questions

Peptide testing, answered.

How do I know a SideChain report is genuine?

Enter the report number and verify code on our verification page, or scan the QR code on the report. You'll see the results exactly as we recorded them. You can also drop the PDF there: we compare its cryptographic fingerprint with the file we released, so any edit — even a single digit — is detected. The file never leaves your device.

What is peptide testing?

Peptide testing is the analytical characterization of a peptide sample — confirming its identity, measuring its purity, and quantifying how much active peptide is actually present. SideChain Analytics performs this by HPLC-MS/MS and reports it as a plain-spoken analytical record.

Which peptides can you test?

36 peptides today on a validated HPLC-MS/MS panel — GLP-1 receptor agonists, growth-hormone-axis peptides, neuropeptides and regenerative compounds among them — and the panel is growing. If a compound isn't listed yet, ask us.

Can you test peptide blends?

Yes. When a sample contains two or more peptides, we resolve and quantify each component on a single HPLC-MS/MS run from one vial — reporting per-component identity and purity, the declared versus measured ratio, and a total active-recovery figure on one consolidated CoA.

Can you test heavy metals and endotoxin too?

Yes. Heavy-metal contaminants by ICP-MS (Agilent 8800 QQQ) and bacterial endotoxin by kinetic chromogenic LAL (USP <85>) both run from the same vial as add-ons, alongside the peptide analysis.

How long does peptide testing take?

Turnaround is 5 to 7 business days after we receive your sample, for the core HPLC-MS/MS identity, purity and net peptide content work. Add-on heavy-metal or endotoxin testing runs from the same vial and can extend that; we confirm timing when we acknowledge receipt.

How do I send a peptide sample to your Ontario lab?

We receive samples at our laboratory at 16 Falconer Drive, Unit 14, Mississauga, Ontario L5N 3M1. Email info@sidechainanalytics.com before shipping and we'll confirm packaging instructions and the right submission path for your work.

How much sample do you need, and what does it cost?

Required sample amount depends on the test and the analyte, so we confirm the quantity and provide a quote once we know what you'd like measured — email info@sidechainanalytics.com with your peptide and the tests you need.

Is SideChain testing for research use only?

Yes. All SideChain analysis is research-use-only and is not for human or veterinary administration. We don't manufacture or sell compounds — our only product is independent data.

Are you independent of peptide sellers and manufacturers?

Yes. We don't manufacture, sell or source compounds and have no commercial interest in the outcome of any analysis. Every result is reviewed by a second analyst before it's released.
