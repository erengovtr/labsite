Services

Three questions. Three orthogonal answers.

Is it the right molecule? How much of it is there? Is anything in it that shouldn't be?

Core panel

Identity, purity & net peptide content

HPLC-MS/MS confirms the molecule by mass, UV chromatography quantifies purity, and net peptide content tells you how much actual peptide is in the powder.

  • Molecular mass confirmation (MS/MS)
  • Chromatographic purity (% area)
  • Net peptide content (% w/w)
  • ~5 business days from receipt

Add-on

Heavy metals by ICP-MS

Trace elemental screening for toxic metals such as lead, cadmium, arsenic and mercury — run from the same vial.

  • Inductively coupled plasma MS
  • Low detection limits
  • Reported against acceptance criteria

Add-on

Bacterial endotoxin (LAL)

Kinetic chromogenic Limulus Amebocyte Lysate assay following USP <85> methodology, reported in EU/mg.

  • Kinetic chromogenic method
  • USP <85> aligned
  • Quantitative EU/mg result

FAQ

Common questions

How long does testing take?

About five business days after we receive your sample for the core HPLC-MS/MS panel. Heavy-metal (ICP-MS) and endotoxin (LAL) add-ons run from the same vial and can extend that timing.

How much sample do you need?

It depends on the tests and the analyte. Tell us what you'd like measured and we'll confirm the quantity along with your quote.

What's the difference between purity and net peptide content?

Purity is the share of the peptide signal that belongs to the target molecule versus related impurities. Net peptide content is how much of the total powder weight is actually peptide — the rest is typically water, counter-ions (like TFA or acetate) and salts. A vial can be 99% pure and still contain only ~80% peptide by weight.

Are you affiliated with any peptide vendor?

No. SideChain Analytics is an independent third-party laboratory. We report what we measure, regardless of who submits the sample.

How can my customers verify my COA?

Each certificate carries a report ID, a private access code and a QR code. Anyone holding the certificate can scan it or enter both on our verification page to see our recorded results. They can also drop the PDF there — we compare its cryptographic fingerprint with the original, so any edit is detected. The file never leaves their device.

Do you test for human use?

No. All testing and reports are for research use only.
