COA verification

Is this certificate real?

Check any SideChain Analytics Certificate of Analysis against our records — and confirm the PDF you were given hasn't been edited since we released it.

Both are printed at the top of the certificate. Scanning the QR code on the COA fills them in for you.

Your file never leaves your device. We compute its SHA-256 fingerprint in your browser and send only that.

How verification works

  1. Record match. Every COA we release is logged with a unique ID and a private access code. Unknown IDs or wrong codes mean the certificate isn't ours.
  2. Tamper check. We keep a cryptographic fingerprint of the exact PDF we released. Change a single digit and the fingerprint no longer matches.
  3. Status. If a report is ever corrected or withdrawn, verification says so — and points to the replacement.
