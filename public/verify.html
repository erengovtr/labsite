Is this certificate genuine?

Check any SideChain Analytics Certificate of Analysis against our records — and confirm the PDF you were given hasn't been edited since we released it. No login required.

Both are printed on the report. Scanning its QR code fills them in for you.

Your file never leaves your device. We compute its SHA-256 fingerprint in your browser and send only that.

How verification works

  1. Record match. Every report we release is logged with its number and a private verify code. Unknown numbers or wrong codes mean the report isn't ours.
  2. Tamper check. We keep the cryptographic fingerprint of the exact PDF we released. Change a single digit and the fingerprint no longer matches.
  3. Status. If a report is ever corrected or withdrawn, verification says so — and points to the replacement.
